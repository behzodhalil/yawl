Privacy

From GitHub we keep your account number, user name and email: to know it is you.

Your panel's variables are sealed in your browser to your own servers: we cannot read them.

Some settings in your panel are kept encrypted with a key on our machine: Git tokens, storage keys and a Hetzner token if you add one. Someone with root on our machine could read those. We do not look at them.

Your panel's database is backed up every night to our storage, encrypted, and kept 14 days.

{{if .Price}}

Your card is given to Dodo Payments, never to us; we keep the customer and subscription numbers Dodo gives us.

{{end}}

We do not sell or share anything about you.
